# Completed local ring at the rank-five circuit

For the explicit circuit tensor in the [statement](proofs/STATEMENT.md),
assume `a,b >= 3`, `a+b >= 7` and `c >= 5`, and put
`q = 2(a+b-6)`, `h = 3a+3b+5c+2`. Its completed local complex algebra is

```text
C[[z_1,...,z_h, x_(i,j): 1<=i<=6, 1<=j<=q]] /
    (x_(i,j)x_(k,l): i != k).
```

It has six regular branches meeting the same smooth reduced
`h`-dimensional germ, dimension `h+q`, depth `h+1`, embedding dimension
`h+6q` and Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity six. Its completed normalization
is the product of the branch rings; its conductor is generated by all
the `x_(i,j)`. In particular, the `6×6×6` witness has dimension 80 and
depth 69. Simultaneous permutations of the tensor factors transport
the formula and the specified witness.

The [proof](proofs/PROOF.md) constructs regular residue coordinates and
one translation that straightens all six branch graphs. Finite
unramified incidence excludes additional limiting branches. This
refines the earlier [rank-five normalization result](../proofs/NORMALIZATION_FIBERS.md).
It gives no homogeneous-vertex depth, global normalization description,
or higher-rank fibre formula.

Run the [exact verification](verification/README.md) from this directory:

```sh
python3 -S -s -B verification/run.py
```

The finite identities and tangent certificates support the written
proof; they do not establish its all-format statement by extrapolation.
