\begin{abstract}
For every $r\geq5$, $a,b\geq3$, $a+b\geq r+2$, and $c\geq r$,
the reduced affine variety of tensors of border rank at most $r$ in
$\C^a\otimes\C^b\otimes\C^c$ fails Serre's condition $S_2$ and is
not Cohen--Macaulay. Its dimension is $D=r(a+b+c-2)$ and its
homogeneous vertex has depth less than $D$. This includes the
rank-five variety in format $3\times4\times5$ and the closure of
the unit-tensor orbit in format $r\times r\times r$ for every
$r\geq5$. The corresponding projective secants are locally
non-Cohen--Macaulay, disproving the universal arithmetic
Cohen--Macaulay conjecture for Segre secants.
Explicit integer tensors have two immersive decompositions.
A rank condition on matrix factors yields smooth target charts with
a codimension-two complement in a birational decomposition space;
the conductor of the finite normalization then obstructs $S_2$.
We prove that rank five is the first rank admitting this
three-factor decomposition mechanism. We also identify the adjacent
Cohen--Macaulay family in format $3\times3\times c$ at rank five
and prove that the mechanism cannot occur on an invertible-slice
chart. Exact arithmetic and a Lean formalization certify the fixed
finite witness calculations; the infinite family follows from the
written proof.
\end{abstract}

\section{The main theorem and its context}\label{sec:introduction}

For positive integers $a,b,c,r$, let
\begin{equation}\label{eq:general-secant}
 X_r(a,b,c)=
 \overline{\left\{\sum_{i=1}^r a_i\otimes b_i\otimes c_i:
    a_i\in\C^a,\ b_i\in\C^b,\ c_i\in\C^c\right\}}^{\mathrm{Zar}},
\end{equation}
with its reduced structure. It is the affine cone over
$\sigma_r(\Seg(\PP^{a-1}\times\PP^{b-1}\times\PP^{c-1}))$.
The coordinate ring is a standard graded domain, since the
parametrizing space is irreducible and its image closure is conical.
All varieties and tensor products in the proofs are over $\C$.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
Suppose that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:main-range}
 r\geq5,\qquad a,b\geq3,\qquad a+b\geq r+2,\qquad c\geq r.
\end{equation}
Set $X=X_r(a,b,c)$, $R=\C[X]$, and $\mathfrak m=R_{>0}$.
Then
\[
 \dim X=D=r(a+b+c-2),
\]
$R$ does not satisfy Serre's condition $S_2$, and $R$ is not
Cohen--Macaulay. Its homogeneous local ring satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:main-depth}
 \depth R_{\mathfrak m}<D=\dim R_{\mathfrak m}.
\end{equation}
There is an explicit integer tensor $T\in X$, given by
\eqref{eq:uniform-witness} and~\eqref{eq:seed-choice}, of ordinary
rank and border rank exactly $r$ for which $\OO_{X,T}$ is not
Cohen--Macaulay. The same assertions hold after permuting the three
factor dimensions.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:examples}
The varieties $X_5(3,4,5)$ and $X_5(6,6,6)$ fail $S_2$, have
respective dimensions $50$ and $80$, and have homogeneous vertex
depths strictly smaller than those dimensions. For every $r\geq5$,
$X_r(3,r-1,r)$ has dimension $2r^2$ and fails $S_2$.
Moreover, if $\Delta_r=\sum_{i=1}^r e_i\otimes e_i\otimes e_i$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:unit-orbit}
 X_r(r,r,r)=\overline{\bigl(\GL_r(\C)\bigr)^3\cdot\Delta_r}
\end{equation}
fails $S_2$ and has vertex depth less than $3r^2-2r$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:projective}
For every format in~\eqref{eq:main-range}, the projective secant
$\sigma_r(\Seg(\PP^{a-1}\times\PP^{b-1}\times\PP^{c-1}))$ has a
non-Cohen--Macaulay local ring at the class of the tensor in
Theorem~\ref{thm:main}. In particular, it is not arithmetically
Cohen--Macaulay.
\end{corollary}

A Noetherian ring $B$ satisfies $S_2$ when
\[
 \depth B_{\mathfrak p}\geq\min\{2,\dim B_{\mathfrak p}\}
 \quad\text{for every }\mathfrak p\in\Spec B.
\]
Cohen--Macaulay rings satisfy $S_2$. Arithmetic Cohen--Macaulayness of
a projective embedding means Cohen--Macaulayness of its homogeneous
coordinate ring, including the affine vertex. It is stronger than
local Cohen--Macaulayness of the projective variety; here both fail.
Theorem~\ref{thm:main} does not specify the exact depth at the witness
or at the vertex. Failure of $S_2$ for $\OO_{X,T}$ may occur at a
further localization and is not an assertion that
$\depth\OO_{X,T}<2$.

\subsection{The proof and the limits of the mechanism}

The proof separates smoothness of suitable target points from the
ambiguity at one explicit tensor. A condition on the ranks of all
subsets of two factor lists forces their matrix span to contain
exactly the expected reduced rank-one points. A proper incidence
argument then reconstructs a smooth local configuration space.
Keeping the third factor list independent gives a smooth
birational decomposition source whose complement of smooth-image
points has codimension at least two.

The integer tensor has two distinct decompositions where addition
is immersive. They produce two distinct open charts in the finite
normalization. If the tensor secant were $S_2$ at that tensor, its
conductor would contain a divisor through the corresponding
normalization point. The codimension estimate excludes such a divisor.
Adding explicit matrices in new rows and columns extends the
construction to every rank in~\eqref{eq:main-range}.

The range in the theorem is sufficient, not a classification of all
non-Cohen--Macaulay secants. We prove that two distinct immersive
$r$-term decompositions cannot occur in three factors for $r\leq4$;
this makes $r=5$ sharp for this mechanism. It does not assert that
all fourth secants are arithmetically Cohen--Macaulay.
The rank-five family $X_5(3,3,c)$ remains Cohen--Macaulay for every
$c\geq1$. We also show why the displayed ambiguity cannot be
transported through an invertible-slice chart to give a Hilbert- or
Quot-scheme counterexample. No global minimal-format assertion,
classification in higher tensor order, or Cohen--Macaulay assertion
about the normalizations is made.
